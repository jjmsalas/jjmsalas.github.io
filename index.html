About the Maker

Craft rooted in tradition, designed for today

I'm James Harwood — a furniture maker and joiner based in the Pacific Northwest. I trained under master cabinetmakers in Vermont before opening my own workshop, where every project starts with a conversation and ends with a piece built to outlast its maker.

My work spans heirloom dining tables, kitchen cabinetry, live-edge slabs, and architectural millwork. I work primarily in American black walnut, white oak, and cherry — sourced from local mills whenever possible.

How It Works

From idea to heirloom

01

Consultation

We discuss your space, how you'll use the piece, your aesthetic preferences, and timeline.

02

Design & Quote

I produce detailed drawings and a written quote within a week. No surprises — materials and labor are itemized.

03

Build

Every joint is cut by hand. I send progress photos throughout the build so you're never in the dark.

04

Delivery

White-glove delivery and installation included. Each piece comes with care instructions and a 10-year structural warranty.
